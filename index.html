Active Programs

Where we
work hardest.

Three flagship initiatives, each tackling a different dimension of the ecological crisis — on land, in water, and in the air.

Forest

Amazon Canopy Restoration Network

Partnering with 14 indigenous communities to restore 200,000 acres of degraded Amazon rainforest using traditional agroforestry methods.

Goal Progress
78%
Ocean

Blue Corridor: Pacific Reef Protection

Establishing a 1,200-mile marine protected zone linking fragmented coral ecosystems across five Pacific island nations.

Goal Progress
54%
Grassland

Northern Grasslands Seed Archive

Preserving 40,000 native seed varieties from North American prairies — the living library of a vanishing biome.
